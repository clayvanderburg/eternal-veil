# Molecular Dance

Glowing molecules tumble through the Void. Bonds shimmer, orbiting lights circle their centers, and each structure breaks apart as the next one assembles.

Molecular Dance is an artistic take on molecules and atoms. Open the console → Presets → Molecular Dance. Try a different palette under Colors to give it another feel.

Found a combination you like? Use Share Scene and leave the link in the comments.

Free to explore at https://eternalvoid.io/.
