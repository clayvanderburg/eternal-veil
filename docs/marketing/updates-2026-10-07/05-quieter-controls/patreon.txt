# More scene. Less interface.

The controls now fade after four seconds without input, so the scene has room to fill the screen. The console-close behavior was also fixed, and automatic updates no longer bring the controls back.

Want to hide them straight away? Open the console and choose Hide controls. Tap the scene or move the pointer when you want them back.

This is a small change, but it makes it easier to watch a configuration without the interface sitting over it.

Explore at https://eternalvoid.io/.
