# Spatial Audio

Spatial Audio has its own panel in Eternal Void. Blend binaural tones with sound that moves between the left and right channels.

Open Music sources → Spatial Audio, then press Play. Change the sound, movement, levels and timing independently. Binaural tones and bilateral movement each have their own switch.

Use stereo headphones for the intended effect and keep the volume comfortable. Audio is optional.

Try it at https://eternalvoid.io/.
