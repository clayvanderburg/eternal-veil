# Nocturnal Drift

Nocturnal Drift is the ambient playlist inside Eternal Void. Six tracks accompany the visuals, including Slow Orbit and Below the Signal.

Open Music sources → Play a playlist, then choose a track. You can skip, seek, shuffle and repeat. Favorite the tracks you want to return to, or make a playlist of your own; those choices stay in this browser.

Headphones make the stereo movement easier to hear. Music is optional, and the app is free to explore at https://eternalvoid.io/.
